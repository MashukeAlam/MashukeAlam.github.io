Summary: Asmaul Husna is designed with user privacy as a top priority. The app is completely ad-free, does not collect any personal data, has no analytics or tracking tools, and functions as an offline application. Network connectivity is solely utilized for optional content updates.

This Privacy Policy describes how Asmaul Husna ("we", "our", or "the App"), available on Android under the package name com.jim.asmaulhusna, handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data.

The App does not require any account creation or registration. We do not intentionally collect or process any personally identifiable information (PII), such as:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Physical address or postal information
  • Location data (neither precise nor coarse)
  • Device contacts, calendars, or call logs
  • Photos, camera, microphone, or media files
  • Financial or payment information
  • User-generated text or messages

2. Advertisements

Asmaul Husna is 100% ad-free. The App does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs or networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.). As a result, no advertising IDs, tracking cookies, or profiling data are collected for ad delivery or behavioral targeting.

3. Analytics and Tracking

We believe in clean, respectful software. The App contains no analytics, diagnostic trackers, or telemetry SDKs (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook SDK). We do not track your in-app actions, viewing history, time spent, or interaction patterns.

4. Offline Use and Network Connectivity

Asmaul Husna is fundamentally an offline application. Once installed on your device, all core functionality and primary content are accessible without an active internet connection.

The only reason the App may use network connectivity is strictly to check for and download content updates (such as new or updated text, corrections, translations, or audio content). During this process:

  • No personal data or device identification is transmitted.
  • The request is made solely to fetch content files from the host server.
  • Standard server access logs (such as IP address sent by the operating system for HTTP/HTTPS requests) may be processed transiently by the content server solely for networking delivery purposes, and are never combined with personal identities.

5. Local Storage and Preferences

Any user preferences, saved bookmarks, or settings that you configure in the App are stored locally on your device using Android's standard local storage mechanisms. This information never leaves your device and is permanently removed if you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

6. Children's Privacy

Asmaul Husna is intended for general audiences of all ages, including families and children. Because we do not collect, retain, or share any personal information from any user, we comply fully with applicable children's privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Third-Party Services

The App does not share your data with any third parties or integrate third-party services that collect user personal data.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on any remote servers, there is no user data stored by us to retain or delete. 9. Security

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy. Since the App does not collect personal data over the internet or maintain remote user databases, your personal information is not exposed to risk through the App.
