FlagFinder

How To Play

Guess the FLAGFINDER in 6 tries.

  • Each guess must be a valid sovereign nation or territory.
  • With each incorrect guess, 1 of the 6 tiles flips away, revealing more of the flag.
  • You will also see the distance and compass direction (e.g., ↗️ North-East) from your guess to the mystery country.

A new mystery flag is available every day!
